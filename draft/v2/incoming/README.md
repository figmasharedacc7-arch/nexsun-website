# Envato image drop

Download from Envato Elements, rename to the filename in the first column, and drop the file
in this folder. Tell me when they are in and I will resize, compress and wire them into the pages.

Any format is fine (jpg, png, webp). Do not resize them yourself, send the largest version
Envato offers and I will handle it.

## Specs that matter

- **Landscape**, at least **2400 x 1350**. Bigger is better, I downsize, never upscale.
- **Subject on one side, quiet space on the other.** These are background images with a cream
  gradient washing across roughly 40 to 66 percent of the frame, and the headline sits in that
  washed area. A photo with the subject dead centre gets its subject covered by text.
  The "side" column below says which side the subject should sit on.
- **No burned-in text, watermarks or recognisable brand logos.**
- **Warm or neutral daylight** reads best against the cream and amber palette. Avoid heavy blue
  or teal grading, and avoid night shots.
- Real photography over 3D renders where you have the choice.
